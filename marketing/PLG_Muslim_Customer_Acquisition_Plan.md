## What to sell to a specific Muslim buyer
Do not claim "Muslims are a niche, therefore this will sell." Pick a concrete need and test demand before building a larger catalogue.

Priority concept to validate: **Salah Starter Companion — a plain-English practice planner for adults learning or rebuilding consistency in salah.**
Possible contents: a prayer-learning progress tracker, personal questions to take to a trusted teacher, a weekly practice planner, and a habit/reflection page. Do not invent Arabic text, transliteration, religious rulings, or claim scholarly approval. Any religious content must be checked against reliable sources or a qualified teacher before sale.

This is a concept to validate, not a claim that sales are guaranteed. Keep the current discipline workbook as a secondary offer unless real buyer feedback proves demand.

## Direct audience test — seven days, no ads
1. Publish one genuinely useful tip daily from the existing workbook: daily anchors, breaking a task into 10 minutes, restarting after missing a day, gratitude through action.
2. Share only in Muslim creator/education spaces whose rules permit promotion. First answer questions and contribute; do not mass-DM people or drop unsolicited links.
3. Ask 5 people in your existing community for feedback, not a fake review: "Would this solve a real problem for you? What is missing? Would you pay for it?"
4. Put one tracked, working product link in the profile/bio where allowed. Check the URL in a private browser before promoting.
5. Record views, profile visits, product-link clicks, sales, and objections each day.
6. If there are no views, fix distribution. If there are views but no clicks, fix the hook/offer. If there are clicks but no sales, fix product fit, preview, trust, or price.
7. Do not buy ads or another tool until this test produces evidence.

## Three value-first posts
### Post 1 — realistic routine
A routine does not have to be complicated to be useful. Choose one anchor for tomorrow, attach it to something you already do, and keep it small enough to repeat. What usually breaks your routine: time, energy, distractions, or not knowing where to start?

### Post 2 — restart without shame
Missing one day does not erase your progress. Resume at the next opportunity and choose the smallest useful step. Consistency is something you practise, not a label you either have or do not have.

### Post 3 — gratitude with action
Gratitude does not mean pretending hardship is easy. Name one blessing, then choose one beneficial action: thank someone, help where you can, or move one responsibility forward.

Only add a product invitation after the post provides value, and only use a real checkout link. Do not claim buyers, testimonials, or outcomes that have not been verified.
