# PLG Gumroad Marketing Execution Sheet
## One product. One audience. One clear next step.

### Product and offer
- Product: **7-Day Discipline Reset — A Practical Muslim Guide to Rebuilding Your Daily Routine**
- Audience: Muslims who want a practical, faith-conscious way to build a routine without perfectionism.
- Test price: **$7–$9** after the downloadable file is uploaded and checked. This is a test, not a sales forecast.
- Promise: seven short daily exercises to set an intention, choose daily anchors, break down a task, protect attention, move a responsibility forward, practice gratitude, and review the week.
- Do not claim scholarly endorsement, guaranteed transformation, or features that are not in the actual download.
- Product file prepared in this repository: `products/7-Day-Discipline-Reset-Workbook.html` (print-ready; open in a browser and use Print → Save as PDF).

### The marketing formula
**Specific struggle → small useful tip → one-step example → honest product invitation → track clicks and sales.**

Don't post "buy my ebook" by itself. Give a genuinely useful step first, then explain that the workbook organizes seven of these steps.

### Three ready-to-use posts

#### Post A — routine feels overwhelming
Trying to rebuild your routine can feel impossible when you try to fix everything at once. Pick one anchor for tomorrow: prepare for the next salah, write down your one most important task, or get one thing ready before bed. Keep it small enough to repeat.  
I made the **7-Day Discipline Reset** to help turn that approach into a simple week of guided exercises. If it fits what you need, the link is in my profile.  
#PlanLifeGrateful #MuslimLifestyle #DailyDiscipline #Gratitude

#### Post B — missed a day
Missing a day doesn't mean you have to throw away the whole plan. Restart at the next opportunity. Ask: "What's the smallest useful step I can take now?" Then do that step without insulting yourself.  
That restart principle is part of the **7-Day Discipline Reset**, a practical workbook from Plan Life Grateful. Find it through the link in my profile.  
#PlanLifeGrateful #Consistency #PersonalGrowth #MuslimCreator

#### Post C — gratitude with action
Gratitude isn't pretending hardship is easy. It can mean naming one blessing and taking one beneficial action in response: thank someone, help where you can, or move one responsibility forward.  
The **7-Day Discipline Reset** gives you a short daily structure for intention, action, and gratitude. Link in profile if you'd like the guided version.  
#PlanLifeGrateful #GratitudePractice #IntentionalLiving

### Seven-day publishing rotation
1. Post A: overwhelm → one anchor.
2. Post B: missed a day → restart without shame.
3. Post C: gratitude → practical action.
4. Share a blank example of the Day 3 "10–20 minute next step" exercise; don't expose buyer information.
5. Ask a genuine question: "What makes routines hardest to maintain—time, energy, distractions, or uncertainty?" Reply helpfully; no spam.
6. Share one lesson from the workbook and invite feedback from people who actually try it.
7. Recap the week's tips and make one clear invitation to the product.

Use the same core message but rewrite it naturally for each platform. Never mass-post identical spam or send unsolicited bulk DMs. Do not use fake testimonials or imply customers exist if none have been verified.
