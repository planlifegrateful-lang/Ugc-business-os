# 7-Day Discipline Reset
## A Practical Muslim Guide to Rebuilding Your Daily Routine

You do not need to change everything at once. Start with one manageable step, build a routine you can repeat, and review your progress with honesty and gratitude.

### What this guide helps you do
- Choose realistic priorities instead of creating an overwhelming list
- Make room for worship, responsibilities, personal growth, and gratitude
- Break a large goal into one useful next action
- Review your week and adjust without giving up

### Your 7-day path
**Day 1 — Set your intention:** Write what matters most and why.
**Day 2 — Choose daily anchors:** Identify a few realistic points in your day.
**Day 3 — Shrink the task:** Turn one big goal into a step you can finish.
**Day 4 — Protect attention:** Identify one distraction to reduce.
**Day 5 — Move a responsibility forward:** Choose one important unfinished task and take a step.
**Day 6 — Practice gratitude:** Record three blessings and one action that reflects appreciation.
**Day 7 — Review and continue:** Keep what worked, simplify what did not, and choose next week's focus.

### Who this is for
Muslims who want a practical, faith-conscious approach to discipline, responsibilities, and gratitude. This is a planning resource, not a religious ruling or a promise of specific results.

### Begin with one step
Choose one useful action today. Repeat it tomorrow. Let consistency grow from there.
